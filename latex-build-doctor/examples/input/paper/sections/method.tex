\subsection{Model}

The steady-state energy balance is
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \alpha \nabla^2 u + q,
\label{eq:balance}
\end{equation}
with the boundary condition
\begin{equation}
\left( \kappa \frac{\partial T}{\partial x} \right)_{x=0} = h (T - T_\infty)
\label{eq:bc}
\end{equation}
and a stored energy of $E = 3.2 J$ measured calorimetrically.
