# Notes from the authors (informal)

- The 100-label setting is the point of the paper; do not present it as a
  convenience subsample.
- The kinase panel is proprietary; we cannot release it, so the contribution must
  not claim generalisation to arbitrary protein families.
- Reviewer two of the earlier submission complained that "the gap is asserted, not
  derived". The three method routes are (i) pretraining objectives, (ii) data
  selection, (iii) encoder and geometric priors.
- We do not have a theoretical guarantee for the coverage score. Say so.
