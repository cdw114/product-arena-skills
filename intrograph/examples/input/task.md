# Task

Write the **Introduction and Related Work** of the paper described in
`manuscript.md`, for submission to a computational-chemistry journal.

Deliver four parts that hang together:

1. **Background** — the research lineage and the specific unsolved problem, not a
   generic statement that the topic is important.
2. **Related Work** — grouped by method route, comparing the groups on the same
   dimensions (method route, data setting, evaluation, conclusion, limitation).
   Not one paragraph per paper.
3. **Research gap** — derived from the limitations of the works you cited, plus one
   sentence on how this paper fills it.
4. **Contributions** — 3–5 bullets, each one traceable to a figure, table, section or
   reported result in the manuscript.

Constraints:

- English, numeric citation style (`[1]`).
- 900–1200 words, suitable to drop into the manuscript as written.
- Do not introduce any number that is not in the manuscript.
- Any statement that is your own judgement rather than a cited finding must be
  marked as such.
- Cite only the works in `library.bib`.
